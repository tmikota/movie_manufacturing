# What's the True cost of a Tool?

- Planning
- Development
- Implementation
- Delivery
- Ongoing Support.

The untracked Cost:

The burn rate of ALL artists dependent on that "tool" or "system".

I have to mow a lawn.  No matter what it has to be done by a certain date.

I have two options:

1. Finger nail clippers - as many as i like and as many people as i can hire.
1. Lawnmower - but we have to build it from scratch.

So what does it cost to create the lawn mower?

The build cost & the difference in labor between the months using the lain clippers and the new cost once one person is using the lawnmower every other week.

Production is Manufacturing.   The budgets seem enormous - it seems like we'll never run out of money, but:

Average Salary: $2000/week

40 artists: $80,000/week

Developer Salary: $3000/week

1 Developer: $300/week

Tool Development Time: 4 weeks

Training Time: 6 months

Total Cost:

( $80,000 + $3000 ) * 28 = $2.8M

If the delivery was at month 3 the studio likely goes out of business or drastically reduces iterations & quality.
