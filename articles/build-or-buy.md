Costs of Internal Development

- Gather Specs from every type of user
- Prototype
- User Testing & Approval
- Support
  - Bugs
  - New Features
- Risk
  - Every Person you hire could have used the software differently.
  - Every show hires new people
  - Every show has new requirements from those new people and from the unique makeup of the show.

That requires a pretty large team.

In animation - there is innovation every year.  So you might need to copy new software every year.

Let's do an example:

Many studios i talk to want to replace tools like shotgrid, ftrack and Ayon with internal software.  It's a very common request.   They think it's a differentiator between them and the competition.  They think it means they've invented their own software.

I don't understand why - you can't patent it.  And if we look at the cost of these tools:

## Off the shelf costs

Shotgrid, Ftrack, Ayon - all cost around $50/user.

They are production trackers - the equivalent of Jira for software development.   They track what people are doing.  You CAN use them to build pipelines.   But that means you have to hire pipelined evelopers for every department in order to use them.

So what we're looking at - is if you want to build your own shotgrid - you've got 1-2 years of doing that and an ongoing support developer for the rest of time - before you even start building departmental pipelines.

Shotgrid tells you schedule and status.   It doesn't provide any automation without building custom tools for it.

This means you can't automate anything for production until you've been in business for 1-2 years.

Costs to buy

The Verdict:

Buy commodities - build innovations.

The exception - if you can truly reduce waste by building and the ROI warrants the expense.
